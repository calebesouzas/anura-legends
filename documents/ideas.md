# Combat
## Gadgets
Helpers, protectors, utilities, accessories, traps.

---

### Possibilities
1. Bombs
    - Just deals damage.
2. Flashbangs
    - Will blind players nearby.
3. Smokes
    - Can visually block corridors, gates and corners.
4. Goo
    - Slows anything down and sticks with small entities.
5. Fire Bomb
    - Deals continuous damage if an enemy stands on it.
6. Wall
    - Can physically block anything almost anywhere.
    - Breaks when gets damaged enough.
7. Mine
    - Like a bomb but explodes when someone passes through
    - Also explodes when gets hit.
8. Medicine Kit
    - Heals allies in it's area.
    - Can be destroyed by enemies.
9. Bucket
    - A trap of plasma that you put in the ceilling.
    - Can be destroyed by enemies.
10. Chain
    - Locks the enemy you hit with it.
